PRIVACY POLICY

Melto Run: Online Multiplayer

Developer: TST Labs

Game: Melto Run: Online Multiplayer

Last Updated: August 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how TST Labs ("we", "us", or "our") handles user privacy and data for the mobile game Melto Run: Online Multiplayer.

---

1. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

Melto Run: Online Multiplayer is designed with privacy in mind and does not require traditional user account creation.

  • Usernames: Players can enter a display username to join online multiplayer servers. This name is visible to other players on the server during match play.
  • IP Address & Network Data: When connecting to our multiplayer servers, standard network connection data (such as temporary IP addresses) is processed automatically to connect you to game servers and prevent network security abuse.
  • No Personal Information Collected: We do not collect personal identifiers such as real names, home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, or precise device location data inside the game.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

Any technical data or display usernames processed by Melto Run: Online Multiplayer are strictly used to:

  • Provide active multiplayer matchmaking and server connections.
  • Maintain server stability and block malicious activity or cheating.

3. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & DATA SHARING

We do not sell, rent, or trade any player data to third parties. We may use third-party server infrastructure providers strictly to host multiplayer matches and route network traffic securely.

4. DATA DELETION REQUESTS

Because Melto Run: Online Multiplayer does not maintain persistent accounts, temporary match session data and entered usernames automatically reset. If you wish to request the deletion of any support emails sent to us, please email supermoniorunsupport@gmail.com.

5. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Melto Run: Online Multiplayer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Parents or guardians who believe personal information was accidentally provided may contact us for prompt removal.
